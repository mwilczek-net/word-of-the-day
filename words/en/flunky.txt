Flunky

A person who performs menial tasks or has a subordinate role; someone who blindly follows orders without question.
Often used to describe a follower who lacks independence or critical thinking.
Can also refer to a uniformed attendant or servant.

Related: toady, lackey, minion, underling, yes-man

Examples:
- He refused to be just a flunky in the organization and demanded a more meaningful role.
- The company was run by the boss and his team of flunkies.
- Don't let them treat you like a flunky — you have valuable skills to offer.
