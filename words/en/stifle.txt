Stifle

To prevent something from developing or being expressed, such as creativity, criticism, or debate.
To hold back a reaction or sound, such as a laugh, a yawn, or a cry.
It can also mean to make it difficult to breathe, especially because of heat or lack of fresh air.

Similar words: suppress, restrain, hold back; smother, suffocate (when referring to breathing).

Examples:
- Too many rules can stifle creativity.
- She tried to stifle a laugh during the meeting.
- The heat in the crowded room was stifling.
