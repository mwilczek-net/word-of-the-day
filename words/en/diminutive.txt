Diminutive

A diminutive is a word or form that expresses small size, affection, or informality.
In grammar, it often uses a suffix (for example, -let or -ie/-y) to create a "smaller" or "dearer" version of a noun.
In everyday use, diminutives can sound warm, playful, or sometimes slightly patronizing, depending on tone and context.

Near equivalents: little form, pet form, endearing form.

Examples:
- "Piglet" is a diminutive form of "pig."
- She called him "Johnny," a diminutive of "John."
- In this dialect, diminutives are common in family conversations.
