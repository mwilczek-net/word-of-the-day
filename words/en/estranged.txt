Estranged

No longer close or in contact with someone who used to be part of your life, such as a spouse, family member or friend. It usually follows a quarrel or a long period of distance, and it often carries a sense of emotional coldness or hurt.

Similar: alienated, separated, distant, disaffected, on bad terms

- He has been estranged from his brother ever since the argument over their father's will.
- She sent a card to her estranged husband, hoping they could finally talk.
- After years of silence, the estranged friends met again at a funeral.

Note: Often used in the phrase "estranged from someone". In legal or news contexts, "estranged wife/husband" means a spouse who is separated but not yet divorced.
