Finicky

Very particular about what you like or accept, especially over small details, and therefore hard to please.
Often used about food, preferences, or someone's standards. It can also describe something that needs careful handling or adjustment to work properly.

Similar words: fussy, picky, particular; fiddly (for tasks or things).

Examples:
- He's a finicky eater who won't touch anything spicy.
- She's finicky about the fit of her shoes.
- The printer is finicky: the paper has to be lined up just right.

Usage: Usually mildly critical when describing a person. "Picky" is a common, more informal alternative.
