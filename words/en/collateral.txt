Collateral

Collateral is something pledged as security for a loan, which a lender can seize if the borrower fails to repay.
It's also used more broadly for a secondary, often unintended, effect or damage caused by an action — as in "collateral damage."

Similar: security, pledge, guarantee / side effect, byproduct

- "She put up her car as collateral for the loan."
- "The bank required collateral before approving the mortgage."
- "The layoffs were collateral damage from the merger."
